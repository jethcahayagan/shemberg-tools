SHEMBERG QA LAB CHEMICALS INVENTORY

UPDATE YOUR EXISTING RECORDS
1. Keep your original JSON backup unchanged.
2. Open Shemberg-Backup-Updater.html in your browser.
3. Choose your existing JSON backup and click Download updated JSON backup.
4. Open Shemberg-Chemicals-Inventory.html. Under Backups & guide, restore the updated JSON.

The updater works locally and never uploads your records. Original quantities, bottle keys and historical logs are retained.

The app has Set Active / Set Old stock buttons, automatic chemical codes, fraction bottle labels and copied editable details when adding a bottle.

Registry-identity-review.csv lists eleven spreadsheet CAS conflicts retained for review; no chemical identity was silently overwritten. New list entries have no quantities until you enter actual stock.
